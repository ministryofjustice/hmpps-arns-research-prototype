Key differences from SAN Community

  • Accommodation: The opening questions now ask about accommodation before custody and use past-tense headings.

    Both settled accommodation answers and the no-accommodation reasons lead to a new question about plans for accommodation on release. Yes continues to who Alex plans to live with. No and Unknown continue to changes to living arrangements on release. Unknown reveals a text field.

    The accommodation summary uses the updated headings and includes the release plans response and any Unknown details.

  • Drug use: A new gate question asks whether there is evidence Alex has been using drugs in custody. Added the hint ‘Consider use from before and during custody.’ below both ‘Which drugs has Alex used?’ and ‘Which drugs did Alex use before custody?’.
  • Alcohol use: The binge or excessive alcohol use question asks about the 6 months before custody.
