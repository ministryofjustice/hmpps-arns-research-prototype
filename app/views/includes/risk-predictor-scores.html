What this score predicts

The All reoffending predictor predicts the likelihood of someone getting a sanction for any recordable offence.

It includes all sexual and all violent offences, but there are other risk predictors that are specifically designed to predict serious, violent and sexual offences.

How we calculate the score

We calculate the score using:

  • static factors only, when we have limited information (a static score)
  • dynamic and static factors, when we have more detailed information (a dynamic score)

This score has been calculated using static factors only.

Static factors are things which cannot be deliberately changed, and include the person's:

  • age when they enter the community caseload
  • current offence
  • total number of sanctions
  • length of offending history

The person’s current legal gender also counts towards the static score.

Dynamic factors are things that can change, and include their:

  • accommodation status
  • employment status
  • relationship status
  • history of domestic abuse
  • regular activities that encourage offending
  • drug use and motivation to tackle drug use
  • alcohol use
  • impulsivity
  • attitudes that support or excuse criminal behaviour

What this score predicts

The Violent reoffending predictor predicts the risk of reoffending for all offences that are classed as violent.

However, the Serious violent reoffending predictor is specifically designed to predict the most serious violent offences. Because of this, the Violent reoffending predictor should be used to identify whether someone is likely to commit offences not covered by the Serious violent reoffending predictor, such as:

  • actual bodily harm, common assault and aggravated assault
  • robbery and assault with intent to rob
  • arson not endangering life
  • criminal damage not endangering life
  • abduction of children
  • intimidation
  • breach of restraining order
  • possessing or use of knives or offensive weapons offences
  • public order and harassment
  • resisting arrest
  • disorderly behaviour
  • drunkenness offences (not including those related to driving)

To check if a specific offence is classed as violent, go to the full list of violent offences (EQUip).

What this score predicts

The Combined serious reoffending predictor predicts seriously harmful offences by combining the scores from other predictors.

A seriously harmful offence involves an event that is life-threatening or traumatic. The victim will find it difficult or impossible to recover physically or psychologically.

Risks combined in this score

The Combined serious reoffending predictor combines 3 different scores:

  • Serious violent reoffending predictor
  • Direct contact – sexual reoffending predictor
  • Images and indirect contact – sexual reoffending predictor

If a person has no history of sexual or sexually motivated offending, we base their score only on their Serious violent reoffending predictor score.

Everyone gets a Serious violent reoffending predictor score, even if they have no history of violent offending.

What this score predicts

The Serious violent reoffending predictor predicts the risk of reoffending for:

  • murder or attempted murder
  • conspiracy to, soliciting, assisting or encouraging murder
  • manslaughter
  • wounding or inflicting grievous bodily harm (GBH)
  • nonfatal strangulation or suffocation
  • offences related to possessing or use of a firearm or imitation firearm
  • aggravated burglary in a dwelling
  • kidnap or false imprisonment
  • arson endangering life
  • criminal damage endangering life
  • aggravated vehicle taking causing death
  • blackmail
  • other non-motoring offences endangering life

It does not predict the following serious offences:

  • cruelty to or neglect of children
  • abandoning children under two years
  • abduction of children
  • terrorism offences

How we calculate the score

We only calculate a Direct contact – sexual reoffending predictor score for men with a known history of sexual or sexually motivated offending.

We calculate the score using the number of current and previous sanctions a person has for:

  • sexual or sexually motivated offences involving contact with an adult
  • sexual or sexually motivated offences involving direct contact with a child
  • non-contact sexual offences

It does not count indecent images of children offences.

We also look at static factors, which are things which cannot be deliberately changed. These include:

  • age when they enter the community caseload
  • total number of sanctions
  • if their current offence was a direct contact offence committed against a stranger victim

How we calculate the score

We only calculate an Images and indirect contact – sexual reoffending predictor score for men with a known history of sexual or sexually motivated offending.

We calculate the score using the number of current and previous sanctions a person has for:

  • indirect child contact offences
  • indecent images of children offences

Indirect contact offences are:

  • causing a child to watch a sexual act
  • causing or inciting a child to engage in sexual activity
  • arranging child prostitution or pornography
  • arranging or facilitating child sexual offences
  • sexual communication with a child
  • grooming

The score puts the person into a risk band:

Risk band Number of sanctions
Low 0
Medium 1
High 2 or more
