What this score predicts

The Direct contact – sexual reoffending predictor predicts the risk of the following offences with an adult victim:

  • rape or attempted rape
  • sexual assault
  • causing a female to engage in sexual activity without consent
  • sex with an adult relative
  • sexual activity with someone with a mental disorder or learning disability, including ‘breach of trust’ offences
  • abduction (under the Sexual Offences 1956 Act)
  • trafficking for sexual exploitation
  • administering a substance with intent to engage in a sexual activity

It also predicts the risk of the following offences with a child victim:

  • rape or attempted rape
  • assault by penetration
  • sexual assault
  • causing a female to engage in sexual activity without consent
  • sexual activity with a child family member
  • inciting a child family member to engage in sexual activity
  • abuse of trust sexual offences
  • abduction (under the Sexual Offences 1956 Act, not the Child Abduction Act 1984)

It does not predict other sexual offences that do not involve actual or attempted physical contact with a live human victim. For example:

  • possession of extreme pornographic images
  • exposure
  • voyeurism
  • sending a photograph or film of genitals
  • sharing or threatening to share an intimate photograph or film

Further guidance about the offences it does not predict is available as an annex to the Understanding risk predictors - actuarial risk assessment instruments (ARAI) user guidance (EQUip).
