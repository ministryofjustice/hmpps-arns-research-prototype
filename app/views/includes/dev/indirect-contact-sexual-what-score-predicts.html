What this score predicts

The Images and indirect contact – sexual reoffending predictor predicts the risk of:

  • causing or inciting a child to engage in sexual activity
  • engaging in sexual activity in the presence of a child under 13 (offender aged 18 or over)
  • causing a child under 13 to watch a sexual act (offender aged 18 or over)
  • meeting a child following sexual grooming
  • sexual communication with a child
  • taking, permitting to be taken or making indecent photographs or pseudo photographs of children
  • distributing or publishing indecent photographs or pseudo photographs of children
  • possession of an indecent photograph of a child
  • possessing prohibited images of children

It does not predict other sexual offences that do not involve actual or attempted physical contact with a live human victim. For example:

  • possession of extreme pornographic images
  • exposure
  • voyeurism
  • sending a photograph or film of genitals
  • sharing or threatening to share an intimate photograph or film

Further guidance about the offences it does not predict is available as an annex to the Understanding reoffending predictors - actuarial risk assessment instruments (ARAI) user guidance (EQUip).
