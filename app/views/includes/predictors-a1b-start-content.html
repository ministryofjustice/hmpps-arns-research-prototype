Use this assessment to better understand {{ offenderFirstName }}’s likelihood of reoffending.

It brings together information about the individual and their offending history to produce reoffending predictor scores.

What this assessment shows

  • Static reoffending predictor scores — based on offending history and other fixed information
  • Dynamic reoffending predictor scores — based on information from an interview with the individual

What you need to complete this assessment

  • Details of the current offence and offending history
  • An interview with the individual is required if you need dynamic reoffending predictor scores

You can still complete the static part of the assessment without an interview.
