Use this analysis to record the risk of serious harm {{ offenderFirstName }} poses, who is at risk, and what could increase or reduce that risk.

Complete a ROSH full analysis after the screening has indicated that one is needed, or when you have additional information that means a full analysis should be done.

What you will record

  • Who is at risk of serious harm and why
  • The nature of the risk
  • When the risk is likely to be greatest, including whether it is imminent
  • Circumstances that are likely to increase the risk
  • Factors that are likely to reduce the risk
  • The current risk of serious harm in the community and in custody
  • Risks to {{ offenderFirstName }}, including suicide, self-harm, coping in custody and vulnerability
  • Other risks, including escape or absconding, control issues and risk to other prisoners
